How do I find the camera URL? +

Most cameras expose RTSP on port 554. The stream path depends on the vendor — try these against your device, substituting user, password and IP:

  • Dahuartsp://user:pass@ip:554/live/ch0
  • Hikvisionrtsp://user:pass@ip:554/Streaming/Channels/101
  • TP-Linkrtsp://user:pass@ip:554/stream1
  • ONVIFrtsp://user:pass@ip:554/onvif1

Generic template → rtsp://user:pass@ip:554/<vendor-path>
